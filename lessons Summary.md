# Lessons: summary

Detailed notes for each example:
- [Retail: slow-moving inventory](lessons-retail.md)
- [Fitness: multi-activity membership](lessons-gym.md)

## What I learned across both examples
- Validation in a separate chat found different problems from the drafting chat.
- Splitting the workflow into stages gave better results than one big prompt.
- Business-model problems (fitness) need questions about partners, pricing and profitability that a software-only checklist misses.
- AI output is a draft. In both examples the final version differs from the first, and the changes are logged at the top of each final file.
- Even after fixing, the requirements still need client approval of thresholds, targets and rules. AI cannot supply those.
